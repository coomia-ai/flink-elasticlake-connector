# Code of Conduct

English | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

This community welcomes participation regardless of background, experience,
identity, or affiliation. Discuss technical disagreements with evidence and respect.

Participants are expected to give constructive feedback, acknowledge mistakes,
respect privacy, and keep discussions relevant. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, personal attacks, sexual misconduct, and publishing private information
without consent are unacceptable.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces
where someone represents the project. Maintainers may remove harmful content,
request corrections, temporarily restrict participation, or ban repeated or severe
violations. Decisions should be proportionate and explained privately when possible.

Report incidents privately to a maintainer using a verified contact on the hosting
profile. Do not post personal details in public issues. A maintainer involved in an
incident must defer review to another maintainer when one is available. Reports
are handled confidentially to the extent possible. The repository is hosted on
[GitHub](https://github.com/coomia-ai/flink-elasticlake-connector); a dedicated
community contact has not yet been configured.
