## Explicit merge gaps / 明确保留的合流缺口

- I-05 independent fixtures do not exist in the upstream checkout. A local manual
  frame example or separate naive test encoder cannot substitute for their acceptance.
- SPEC26 specifies connector-exact-v2 frame lengths, order and endian encoding.
  The request key is described as a SHA over fields, but its exact preimage layout
  is not specified. Preserve supplied original RootFreeFact keys and C; do not
  invent a delimiter/length encoding or claim registered wire/profile capability.
- Source coverage algorithms remain unsupported. Consume the existing
  SourceCoverageProvider port and check the exact canonical epoch frame; no new
  coverage digest, offset comparator, key algorithm or serializer is implemented.
- The 90s/4096, two-manager 180s/8192 and retirement rules can be exercised as pure
  models. Real F20 routing, continuous progress, authoritative inventory, deployment
  watchdog and actual IO exit remain separate gates.
